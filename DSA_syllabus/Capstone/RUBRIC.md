# Capstone Rubric (100 points)

| Category | Points | What excellent looks like |
|----------|--------|---------------------------|
| Data quality & EDA | 25 | Cleaning justified; charts answer questions; balance checked |
| Modeling & evaluation | 30 | ≥2 models; correct split; right metrics; error discussion |
| Code clarity | 15 | Readable, reproducible; Pipeline where useful |
| Demo & presentation | 20 | Clear story; live demo; business language |
| Insight & responsibility | 10 | Actionable insight + bias/limitation note |

## Level guide
- **Excellent:** meets all bullets in the track brief
- **Developing:** works but thin narrative or weak comparison
- **Weak:** incomplete evaluation or broken reproducibility

## Policy (edit for your school)
- Pairs share grade unless contribution note says otherwise
- Late presentation: −10% per day (facilitator discretion)
